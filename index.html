M227 programme entry summary

Calculation notes & workbook review points

This app follows the v8 OSC worksheet formulas for RPM, current, level allocation, wire feed and arc energy. Level 1 includes rotation delay and the last level includes overlap. The standard workbook summary incorrectly duplicates total time in its TIME/LEVEL field; this app shows base time and individual level times separately. The OSC WPS status formula references an adjacent cell rather than the displayed maximum; this app checks the entered limits against every estimated level. The workbook explanation tab and dashboard disagree on equal level allocation and wire feed; the programme tables take precedence. Oscillation spacing is an advisory comparison from the workbook, not a compliance determination.
